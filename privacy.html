PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

1. Overview

Should I Do It? ("we", "our", or "the site") provides a decision-making tool designed to help users think through purchases and other personal decisions.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, and how third-party services may process information.

2. Information You Enter

The calculator may ask you to enter information such as your decision, purchase price, monthly income, savings, debt, decision category, and how badly you want to make the decision.

Please do not enter passwords, banking credentials, credit card numbers, government identification numbers, or other highly sensitive personal information.

3. AI Recommendations

When you request an AI recommendation, information necessary to generate that recommendation may be sent to a third-party AI service provider. We use this information to generate the recommendation requested by you.

We do not ask you to provide your name, email address, bank account information, or credit card information to use the calculator.

4. Analytics

We may use analytics services to understand how visitors use the website, such as page views, device information, approximate location, referral sources, and interactions with the website.

Analytics are used to improve the website and understand overall usage. We do not intentionally use analytics to identify individual users.

5. Advertising

We may display advertisements from third-party advertising providers, including Google AdSense.

Advertising providers may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or similar technologies to provide and measure advertisements. These providers may use information about visits to this and other websites as permitted by their policies and applicable law.

Google provides information about how it uses data on partner websites at How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps .

6. Cookies

The website and third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies for functionality, analytics, security, and advertising.

You can control or block cookies through your browser settings. Some website features may not function correctly if certain cookies are disabled.

7. Data Retention

We aim to collect and retain only information necessary to operate, maintain, secure, and improve the website and its services.

Information processed by third-party services may be subject to those providers' own privacy policies and retention practices.

8. Third-Party Services

The website may use third-party services for hosting, AI processing, analytics, advertising, security, and other technical functions.

These providers may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

9. Children's Privacy

The site is not specifically directed toward children. Users should not submit sensitive personal information through the calculator.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services, advertising, analytics, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or concerns, please contact us through the contact information provided on the website.
